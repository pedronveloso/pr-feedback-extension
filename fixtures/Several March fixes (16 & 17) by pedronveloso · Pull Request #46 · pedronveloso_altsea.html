      Removing Modifier.weight(1f) from the text Column inside the Row means the Column is no longer constrained to fill the remaining horizontal space after the Checkbox. In a Row, without weight, the Column will try to take its intrinsic width, which can cause long text to overflow or not wrap correctly on narrower screens. Consider keeping .weight(1f) on the Column so text wraps within the available space.
